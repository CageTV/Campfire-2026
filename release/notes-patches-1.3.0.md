**Campfire 2026 - Patches 1.3.0** - the same single installer (asks **Regular** or **ESL**, then which patches), with one patch added.

**NEW**
- **Utenlands Nordic Tents (ESL build only)** - the Campfire addon of Utenlands Nordic Tents (Nexus 121203: craftable Nordic tents) for the ESL Campfire. The original plugin links all 37 Campfire records it uses (tent keywords, tent markers and their cell) by the regular FormIDs; with the ESL Campfire those point at other records or at nothing, so the tents cannot be pitched properly. The ESL copy has only those references renumbered (each one checked against both Campfire.esm files). **Selected for you** when you pick the ESL build and the Utenlands addon plugin is active. With the regular build nothing is installed: the original plugin is already right.

**CHANGED**
- README: in Mod Organizer 2 this mod needs a higher priority than the mods whose patch plugin it replaces (Embers XD, Lanterns of Skyrim II, Hunterborn, Use Those Blankets, Utenlands...), or their original copy wins and the ESL patch does nothing.

The thirteen patches of 1.2.0 are byte-identical. The plugins are the original authors' work; the ESL copies only have their Campfire references renumbered.
