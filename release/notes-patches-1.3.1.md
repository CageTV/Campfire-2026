**Campfire 2026 - Patches 1.3.1** - the installer now ticks the patches for the mods you have.

**CHANGED**
- **Every patch is selected for you** when the mod it is for is active in your load order (its plugin: Creation Club Fishing, Dynamic Activation Key, Simple Hunting Overhaul, Embers XD, Northern Roads, Use Those Blankets, Woodcutting Tweaks, and so on). You can still untick any of them.
- The patches that normally come inside the mod they patch (Embers XD heat sources, Lanterns of Skyrim II, Hunterborn, Immersive Barrels, Simple Food and Hunting Overhaul, Skills of the Wild, Utenlands Nordic Tents) are ticked only when you pick the **ESL** build: with the regular build you already have that file from the mod itself.

All patch plugins are byte-identical to 1.3.0.
